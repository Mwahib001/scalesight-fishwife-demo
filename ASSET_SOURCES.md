The theme's `theme--critical.css` identifies Recoleta and Albert Sans. Recoleta is a licensed commercial font; it has not been copied. Petrona (SIL Open Font License), already referenced by the storefront, supplies the local editorial serif fallback. Albert Sans (SIL Open Font License) is self-hosted from the official storefront's public files. Both licenses are retained in `public/fonts`.

Theme colors use the cream / ink relationship visible in the public theme and sampled packaging accents, adjusted for planning readability. They are demo interpretations, not an official published Fishwife palette. No generated Fishwife illustrations are used.

Optional assets to supply: licensed Recoleta webfont, authorized Fishwife wordmark, single-product images for TUN-OO, TUN-SG, SAR-HP and MAC-CHILI. Missing product imagery is visibly labeled and does not prevent the 11-route demonstration.
