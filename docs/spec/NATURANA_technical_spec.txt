 Executive summary
 Build a prospect-specific Size-to-Buy Planning Workspace for the NATURANA × Understatement capsule: eight products and all 85 public variants across
 band/cup, EU numeric and alpha sizing. The experience follows translated size demand through early launch sales, fit-related movement, inventory exposure,
 next-buy allocation and specialist review.

 Public catalog records are supplied facts; operating values are synthetic demonstration assumptions. The workspace is the interface to a managed planning
 intelligence service. ScaleSight maintains analysis and prepares recommendations; NATURANA makes purchasing and commercial decisions.

 Wahib must implement the ten supplied routes, exact page copy, complete dataset and canonical scenarios. Stephanie should understand the downstream
 inventory problem within two minutes and why ScaleSight remains involved after implementation.

 Once customers begin buying through two different sizing systems, what does that demand mean for the next inventory commitment?

 05. Size-system architecture
 The real sizing architecture we can safely build around
 There are three genuine commercial size structures inside one eight-product capsule:

 Band + cup: 70A-95D / 75A-90D/E depending on style.

 EU numeric bottoms: 36-48.

 Alpha: XS-3XL for both the Candy Pink and Cherry sets.

 This is exactly why the demo can feel sophisticated without inventing complexity.

 NATURANA publicly tells customers to derive bra sizing from underbust + bust measurements, uses waist measurements for bottoms, and specifically
 explains sister-size equivalence-for example, 85B and 80C have the same cup volume.

 Understatement likewise builds its guide around below bust, chest, waist and hip measurements. Its public bralette guidance also demonstrates that the
 relationship is not one-to-one: it says a 75C could wear either L or M+, with M+ providing extra cup depth/support.

 That gives us a defensible conceptual model:

 Historical NATURANA size

 → common measurement / fit cohort

 → new commercial size

 → observed sell-through + exchanges

 → updated size curve

 → next-buy decision

 But the actual probability mapping will be explicitly synthetic demo logic, not presented as official NATURANA or Understatement fit guidance.

 Matching-set structure
 The demo should treat the assortment as four real sets:

 07. Embedded planning story
 The fictional operating story is intentionally simple enough to understand quickly but sophisticated enough to show what ScaleSight adds.

 What happened
 The collaboration introduced 85 public product variants across eight products using three commercial sizing structures:

 band + cup, EU numeric and alpha sizing.

 Before launch, the synthetic ScaleSight planning model creates an initial size curve by translating historical NATURANA-style demand through a common
 body/fit layer.

 Once early launch demand arrives, the pattern begins to diverge.

 Three meaningful things happen:

 M demand is stronger than originally translated.

 Some L gross sell-through is being inflated by fit-related exchanges.

 Matching bottoms become constrained sooner than their corresponding tops in selected M sizes.

 That creates the first real ScaleSight decision cycle.

 The question is no longer:

 Which size does the customer choose?

 It becomes:

 Which style × size combinations deserve more inventory commitment, and which apparent signals should we avoid overreacting to?

 Appendix J. Calculation formulas
 Weeks of cover
 weeksOfCover =
 onHand / forwardWeeklyDemand


 Lead-time demand
 leadTimeDemand =
 forwardWeeklyDemand * leadTimeWeeks


 Safety stock
 safetyStock =
 forwardWeeklyDemand * safetyStockWeeks


 Required inventory
 requiredInventory =
 leadTimeDemand + safetyStock


 Reorder gap
 reorderGap =
 max(
   0,
   requiredInventory
   - currentAvailableInventory
   - confirmedIncomingBeforeRequirementDate
 )


 Projected inventory
 projectedInventory[t] =
 openingInventory
 + cumulativeIncoming[t]
 - cumulativeForecastDemand[t]
